How it works

Three taps to learning

🧸

Pick up a number

Soft plush toys — numbers 0 to 9, sized just right for little hands.

📱

Tap it to your phone

Hold the toy near the top of the phone. NFC does the rest — no scanning, no codes.

🎮

Play and learn

Counting, adding, subtracting — right there on screen. Fun enough that they'll ask to play again.

Works on Android phones today. iPhone support coming soon

For parents

Why parents love it

⚡

Zero setup

NFC chips are pre-programmed and sewn inside every toy. No app to download, no account to create, no pairing. Tap and go.

🎯

Screen time with purpose

The toy stays in their hands. Mathmaties bridges physical play and digital learning in a way flashcards and apps simply can't.

📈

Grows with them

Starts with number recognition and counting. Builds up to addition and subtraction. More game modes are on the way.

Built by a family, for families

Mathmaties started at a kitchen table in Melbourne. Moira — developer and mum — had a simple idea: what if learning numbers felt like playing with a toy? Two hackathons and a few prototypes later, Mathmaties was born. We're a family project, not a startup — building this carefully, with real kids in the loop.
